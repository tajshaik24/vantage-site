Privacy Policy

Vantage · Effective November 20, 2026

Vantage is a remote desktop app for iPad and iPhone. It is built so that your information stays between your device and your own computers. The developer does not collect, store, or receive any of your data.

What stays on your device

  • The Macs you save: their names, addresses, and usernames.
  • Your Mac passwords and pairing codes, kept in the device's keychain.
  • A small picture of each Mac's screen from the end of your last session.
  • Your preferences, such as the toolbar's position.

This information never leaves your device except as described below. Deleting a Mac in Vantage deletes its saved information, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

Connections to your Macs

Vantage connects directly to the Macs you choose, using the Screen Sharing built into macOS. Your screen, keystrokes, and clicks travel only between your device and that Mac. Vantage does not record or keep your keystrokes.

Finding Macs on your network

To list nearby Macs, Vantage looks for Macs that announce Screen Sharing on your local network. This happens only on your network. Nothing about it is sent anywhere else.

Tailscale

If you turn on Tailscale, Vantage runs a Tailscale client inside the app and signs in to your Tailscale account. That client exchanges the information Tailscale needs to connect your devices with Tailscale's coordination service, such as this device's name, operating system, and network addresses. Vantage turns off the Tailscale client's diagnostic log upload, so it doesn't send diagnostic logs to Tailscale. This is governed by Tailscale's privacy policy. If you leave Tailscale off, Vantage does not contact Tailscale.

No tracking, ads, or analytics

Vantage contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Children

Vantage does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
